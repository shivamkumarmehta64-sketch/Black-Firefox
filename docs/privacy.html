Privacy & FAQ

Short version: free, open source, no ads, no data selling. Long version below.

Privacy policy

The apps. ytube, mtube, Black Browser and Electronics Calculator do not collect personal data, do not phone home, and store everything locally on your device. No accounts required.

This website. This site uses PostHog, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count anonymous page views and see which pages are useful. Session recording, heatmaps and dead-click capture are disabled, and no personal information is collected. You can block analytics in your browser or with an ad blocker at any time.

Cost. Everything here is free forever. There are no paid plans, no in-app purchases and no subscription tiers.

Ads. This site and every app are completely ad-free. The apps also block most ads inside YouTube via SponsorBlock.

Open source. All source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can verify exactly what the software does.

Frequently asked questions

Is everything really free?

Yes. Every app is free, open source and ad-free. No hidden costs, ever.

Why do I see a certificate warning when I run the app?

The apps are unsigned (code-signing certificates cost money). Windows may warn you — that's normal. You can trust the app, or see the repo README for the certificate hash and verify it yourself.

Do mtube and ytube require an account?

No. They wrap YouTube Music / YouTube in a WebView2 window; you sign into Google inside the app if you want your playlists, but no separate account is needed.

When is Linux support coming?

Linux builds for ytube and mtube are planned. Follow the repos on GitHub for updates.

What is WebView2?

Microsoft's modern browser engine used by the Windows apps. It's preinstalled on Windows 11 and free to install on Windows 10.

How do I uninstall?

The apps are portable — just delete the folder you extracted them to, plus any shortcuts.
