WHAT YOU GET

Browsing that feels like Edge

The familiar workflow you expect from a modern browser, in a fraction of the footprint.

🪶
Ultra-lightweight

A fraction of the size and RAM of Electron-based browsers.

🛡️
3-layer ad blocker

Triple-layered filtering to keep pages clean and loading fast.

🗔
New window (Ctrl+N)

Open a separate browser window the way you expect — ideal for split-screen work.

⭐
Bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B)

A persistent favorites bar right under the address bar — stored locally, never synced.

📖
Reader mode (Ctrl+Shift+R)

Strip ads and clutter for clean, distraction-free article reading.

🔗
Background tabs

Ctrl+click or middle-click a link to open it in a background tab without losing your place.

⚡
Native speed

C# + Windows APIs for instant startup, low memory and non-blocking favicon loading.

🪟
Windows 11 chrome

Edge-sized toolbar, tabs and controls tuned for Windows 10/11 — familiar and easy to click.

🆓
Free & open source

Fully open source, ad-free and free — yours to use and improve.
